SangeRly berisi ribuan berkas kecil (R, paket, IQ-TREE, trimAl). Antivirus memindai satu per satu sehingga program lambat dibuka. Sebagian antivirus juga salah mengira program seperti IQ-TREE atau trimAl berbahaya, karena keduanya program baris perintah tanpa tanda tangan digital.
SangeRly contains thousands of small files (R, packages, IQ-TREE, trimAl). Antivirus programs scan each of them, which makes the start slow. Some antivirus programs also report a false alarm on programs such as IQ-TREE or trimAl, because they are unsigned command-line tools.
SangeRly is an R Shiny application for Sanger sequencing and the sequence analyses that follow it: assembly of forward and reverse reads, species identification with BLAST, multiple sequence alignment and trimming, phylogenetic trees (neighbour-joining and IQ-TREE), figures of the trees, population structure (haplotypes, diversity, neutrality tests, AMOVA / FST, networks, Structure-style clustering), genetic distances and the barcode gap, primer mining and design, and the preparation of files for GenBank.

SFS and linkage (LD)
SFS and linkage (LD): choose the sequences (all or one population), the maximum number of sites and press 'Run SFS and LD'. The site frequency spectrum (folded) is compared with the neutral expectation (an excess of singletons = expansion / purifying selection). LD gives D, D', r2 and Fisher's exact test for every pair of biallelic sites, ZnS (mean r2), Za (adjacent sites), ZZ and Rm (minimum number of recombination events, four-gamete test), plus the plot of r2 against distance.
SFS dan linkage (LD): pilih sekuens (semua atau satu populasi), jumlah situs maksimum, lalu tekan 'Run SFS and LD'. Spektrum frekuensi situs (terlipat) dibandingkan dengan harapan netral (kelebihan singleton = ekspansi / seleksi pemurni). LD memberi D, D', r2, dan uji eksak Fisher untuk setiap pasang situs biallelic, ZnS (rerata r2), Za (situs berdekatan), ZZ, dan Rm (jumlah minimum peristiwa rekombinasi, uji empat gamet), serta grafik r2 terhadap jarak.
